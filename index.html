How this is calculated
  • Wages = basic. With the 50% rule, PF and gratuity use at least half of CTC.
  • Employer PF is 12% of the PF base. Gratuity builds up at 15/26 of a month's wages a year.
  • ESI applies automatically if monthly pay is ₹21,000 or less.
  • New regime: ₹75,000 standard deduction, no tax up to ₹12 lakh taxable, 4% cess.
  • Bonus is taxed at your top rate. Your monthly figure excludes it.
  • It is an estimate. Your payslip depends on your employer and state.
